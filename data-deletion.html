Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This page explains how users of Snake Merge: Color Puzzle ("the App") can request deletion of data associated with their use of the App. The App is developed and operated by an independent developer under the name Creatone Games.

1. How to request data deletion

To submit a data deletion request, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to creatonegames@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request - Snake Merge.
  3. State that you want data associated with Snake Merge: Color Puzzle to be deleted.
  4. Include any available information that may help us identify the relevant data, such as your device platform, approximate installation date, country, support request details, or an identifier displayed by the App.

Do not send passwords, payment card information, identification documents, or other sensitive personal information.

2. What data may be deleted

Depending on the data available and whether it can be linked to your request, deletion may include:

  • Gameplay and analytics events associated with an identifiable app or device identifier.
  • Technical or diagnostic information controlled directly by the Developer.
  • Support correspondence and information you previously sent to us.
  • Marketing or attribution information that can reasonably be connected to your request.

The App does not require account registration and does not ask for your name, phone number, or personal profile. Some gameplay and analytics information may be stored only under pseudonymous or anonymous identifiers. If no identifier is available, it may not be technically possible to connect anonymous records to a specific user.

3. Data stored on your device

You can delete locally stored game data directly from your Android device:

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Open Apps or Applications.
  3. Select Snake Merge: Color Puzzle.
  4. Open Storage.
  5. Select Clear data or Clear storage.
  6. Uninstall the App if you no longer wish to use it.

Clearing the App's storage may permanently remove local progress, settings, currency, and other information stored only on your device.

4. Advertising and analytics providers

The App uses third-party services for analytics, advertising, attribution, and purchase processing. These services may process information as independent data controllers under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • Meta Audience Network
  • AppsFlyer
  • Google Play Billing

We will delete data under our direct control when it can be identified. However, we may not be able to directly delete information independently controlled by third-party providers. You may also need to use the privacy request or account controls provided by those companies.

You can reset or delete your Android advertising identifier through your device's privacy or Google advertising settings. Menu names may differ depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

5. Purchase records

Payments and purchase records are processed by Google Play. Creatone Games does not receive or store your complete payment card information.

Certain purchase and transaction records may be retained by Google or by the Developer where required for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, tax, or other legal obligations.

6. Processing time

We will review your request and respond within the period required by applicable law. We generally aim to process valid deletion requests within 30 days.

We may contact you for additional information if it is reasonably necessary to identify the relevant data or verify that the request is legitimate.

7. Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained where necessary to:

  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Resolve payment disputes or enforce legal rights.
  • Maintain records showing that a deletion request was completed.

Any retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

8. Contact

For questions or data deletion requests, contact: creatonegames@gmail.com.
